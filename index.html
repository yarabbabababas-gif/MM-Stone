WHO WE ARE

Corporate Profile & Infrastructure

Advanced processing facilities located in Hosur and Chennai supplying certified natural stones across global markets.

At Malvigano Mac Pvt. Ltd., we specialize in the comprehensive processing, precision calibration, and international export of premium granite, luxury marble, engineered quartz, and porcelain slabs.

Backed by cutting-edge multi-wire saws and automated resin-polishing lines across Hosur and Chennai, and paired with active extraction partnerships in premier quarry belts across India, Brazil, and Europe, we deliver stones tailored for high-volume commercial towers, bespoke residences, and monumental architecture.

Every shipment undergoes rigorous dry-lay matching, digital deflection tests, and seaworthy ISPM-15 export crating for sea transport.

WHAT WE OFFER

Architectural Material Portfolio

Select from our collection of natural granites, luxury vein-matched marbles, quartz, and heavy structural slabs.
Granite

Natural Granite Slabs

Dense crystalline stone offering outstanding compressive strength and resistance to harsh weather. Highly suited for exterior ventilated cladding, heavy-traffic flooring, and countertops.

Thickness: 18mm, 20mm, 30mm, Custom
Surface Finishes: Polished, Flamed, Leathered, Honed
Application: Flooring, Commercial Facades, Patios
Marble

Luxury Veined Marble

Carefully hand-selected metamorphic stones featuring vivid mineral striations and book-match continuous vein symmetry, curated for luxury residential lounges and upscale hotels.

Thickness: 16mm, 18mm, 20mm
Surface Finishes: Mirror Polished, Soft Honed, Antiqued
Application: Wall Panels, Bathrooms, Foyers
Quartz

Engineered Quartz Slabs

Formulated with 93% pure quartz and polymer resins to deliver impervious, non-porous surfaces. Exceptional stain, scratch, and chemical resistance for kitchens and sterile environments.

Thickness: 15mm, 20mm, 30mm
Surface Finishes: High Gloss Polish, Suede Velvet
Application: Kitchen Islands, Vanity Tops, Food Prep
Porcelain

Tiles & Porcelain Slabs

Ultra-large format sintered porcelain slabs manufactured under 15,000+ tons of pressure. Heatproof, zero water absorption, and immune to UV discoloration in indoor and outdoor settings.

Dimensions: 3200x1600mm, 2400x1200mm
Thickness: 6mm, 9mm, 12mm
Application: Seamless Cladding, Exterior Facades
Monument

Thick Slab & Monument

Heavy structural granite blocks and oversized thick-sawn slabs calibrated for memorial monuments, carved architectural pedestals, colonnades, and landscape curbstones.

Thickness: 50mm up to 250mm+
Machining: Gangsaw, Diamond Wire Saw, CNC
Application: Monuments, Keystones, Public Plazas
Turnkey

Turnkey Project Sourcing

Comprehensive material takeoff, block reservation, batch matching, and container load optimization for commercial developers, architectural studios, and contractors worldwide.

Incoterms: FOB, CIF, CFR, Door Delivery
Documentation: Certificate of Origin, ISPM-15, Packing List
Logistics: Ocean Containers via Chennai Ports
